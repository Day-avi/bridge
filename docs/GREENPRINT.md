# Bridge — Greenprint (Idea & Core Values)

## One-line idea
Bridge helps Instagram product businesses attract the right buyers and automate the path from Reel to delivered order.

## Who it is for
Small Instagram-first sellers of physical products: clothing, handicrafts, jewellery, art, beauty, home décor. Start with India; expand globally later.

## Problems
1. Reels reach the wrong audience; engagement does not become sales.
2. Abusive, sexual or spam comments drain the owner.
3. Content, orders and shipping live in disconnected tools.
4. Owners cannot tell which content produces delivered revenue.

## Core loop (the product's moat)
Reel -> relevant reach -> buyer-intent comment/click -> checkout -> shipment -> delivered or returned -> revenue signal -> next strategy.

## Core values
- Honest: never claim to "beat the algorithm". We improve signals and measure results.
- Outcome over vanity: optimise for relevant enquiries and delivered revenue, not views.
- Creator safety: abusers are responsible for abuse. Protect owners without silencing them.
- Human in control: risky actions (public replies, messages, refunds) need approval at first.
- Official APIs only: no scraping, no password handling, minimum permissions.
- Own the intelligence, rent the plumbing: use Shopify, Shiprocket, Meta as sources of truth.
- Small and maintainable: one codebase, managed services, no team required.
- Test every phase before the next.

## Non-goals (for now)
Own payment gateway, own courier network, Shopify replacement, native apps, every country's customs/tax, autonomous selling without approval, other social networks.

## Success metrics
Relevant-comment share, enquiries per 1,000 reached accounts, content-attributed delivered orders, abusive comments hidden, owner hours saved, 30-day retention.

## Honest risks
Meta App Review and access limits; recommendation quality unproven until pilots; cross-border complexity; AI mistakes in moderation.
